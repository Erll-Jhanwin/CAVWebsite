Our Story

More Than a
Studio. More Than

CAV was born from a simple belief: creativity thrives when you're comfortable. We fused a professional photography studio with a warm, artisan café so your best work happens naturally.

From intimate portraits to product shoots, from morning espresso to afternoon sessions — everything under one roof, designed for makers.
